A note about these stories

These diagrams distinguish mind-born creation, descent, marriage, and divine parentage rather than treating every connection as biological. They present selected accounts, not one definitive genealogy; relationships and lists can differ across texts and regional traditions. Where generations are omitted, the diagram labels the connection as descent.

For Brahma's mind-born children, see Bhagavata Purana 3.12.4 and 3.12.21–24. Other references include Cantos 4, 8 and 10; the Valmiki Ramayana, Bala Kanda and Ayodhya Kanda; and the Mahabharata, Adi Parva.
